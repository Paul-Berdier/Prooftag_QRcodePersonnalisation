E044 HOTFIX 2 — HPS provenance + scoring recovery

Observed runtime failure:
  QualityProvenanceError: PEP 610 VCS commit is absent or invalid

Cause:
- HPSv2 was correctly downloaded from the immutable codeload URL for commit
  866735ecaae999fa714bd9edfa05aa2672669ee3.
- Because pip installed a local /tmp tarball, its direct_url.json recorded an
  archive/file installation instead of vcs_info.commit_id.
- quality_scoring.py intentionally fails closed when that immutable VCS revision
  cannot be proven.

Fix:
1. Dockerfile records the exact HPS source commit into the installed
   hpsv2-1.2.0.dist-info/direct_url.json and immediately validates it through
   the same _installed_distribution_source() function used at runtime.
2. A recovery script reuses the already-complete p01 Stage1/Stage2 and all
   18 SR-MPGD checkpoints, rerunning ONLY QR/quality scoring and finalization.
3. e044_aggregate.py repairs stale /attempts/... image/latent paths after the
   atomic move to /prompts/<prompt-id>. This prevents a later final aggregation
   failure.

No scientific parameter changes.
No Stage1/Stage2/SR-MPGD recomputation is required for the failed p01 attempt.
